Everything you need, nothing you don't

Structured Reviews

No free-text rants. Just clear, predefined tags — paid on time, payment delayed, good communication — so review data stays consistent and fair.

Professional Invoicing

Create and send clean invoices in minutes. Track payments, due dates, and outstanding balances without spreadsheets.

Privacy by Design

Client phone numbers are never stored in plain text. Cashevide matches clients using one-way hashing — never exposing personal data.

Open Source

Read the code, audit how it works, or self-host it yourself. No black boxes, no vendor lock-in.

Built on trust, not gossip.

Traditional review platforms invite public call-outs, legal risk, and messy free-text opinions. Cashevide takes a different approach — structured categories instead of open rants, hashed identifiers instead of exposed phone numbers, and a system designed to protect freelancers without putting anyone at risk.
